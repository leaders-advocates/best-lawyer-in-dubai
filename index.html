How to Choose the Best Lawyer in Dubai

The best lawyer in Dubai is not the biggest name. It is the licensed advocate who knows your type of case and the court that will hear it.

Dubai has hundreds of law firms. Many of them work only on company deals. Do you have a divorce, a rental dispute or a police case? Then you need a team that goes to court for that every week.

Use the checklist below before you sign anything. It works for any firm, including ours.

Quick Summary

Only licensed advocates can represent you before the onshore UAE courts, and hearings run in Arabic. So check the licence first. Then ask how many cases like yours the lawyer has handled in the last year. Ask who will work on your file, how often you will get updates, and what the full fee will be. Read Google reviews from real clients. A good lawyer also tells you when a case is weak, before you pay. Where Your Case Goes

Dubai Courts and Authorities We Work With

Many cases are lost because they start in the wrong place. Here is where common disputes are heard in Dubai.

Family and Divorce

Cases start at the Family Guidance section of Dubai Courts, then move to the Personal Status Court. Muslims and non-Muslims follow different personal status laws.

Inheritance

Estate claims between heirs go to the Probate Court, set up by Dubai Decree No. 25 of 2023. Wills may be registered in Dubai or the DIFC.

Rent and Tenancy

Landlord and tenant disputes go to the Rental Disputes Center. A registered Ejari contract is usually needed to file.

Employment

Workers first file a complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). If it is not settled, the case goes to the Labour Court.

Criminal and Cybercrime

Cases begin with Dubai Police, then the Public Prosecution decides whether to send them to the Criminal Court.

Property and Commercial

Ownership, contract and business disputes go to the Dubai Courts: First Instance, Appeal and Cassation. Some contracts choose arbitration instead.
